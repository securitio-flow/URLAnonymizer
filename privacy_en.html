Privacy Policy

Automatically remove tracking, marketing and referral parameters (UTM, fbclid, gclid and others) from URLs to improve your privacy. The extension does not collect any personal data.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the URL Anonymizer browser extension processes your data. Our primary goal is to protect your privacy while providing a simple and secure browsing experience.

1. Data Collection and Processing

The extension does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information, browsing history, IP addresses or any other information about your online activity. All URL cleaning operations are performed locally within your web browser on your own device.

2. Network Communication

The extension does not make any network requests to external servers. It works completely offline and does not require an Internet connection to perform its functionality.

3. Local Storage (Chrome Storage)

The extension uses chrome.storage.local exclusively to store your preferences, including:

  • selected URL cleaning mode,
  • allowlist (whitelist) of domains,
  • custom rules.

These settings remain stored only on your device and are never transmitted or synchronized with third-party services.

4. Required Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its operation.

Permissions Used

  • declarativeNetRequest / webNavigation – required to safely and efficiently remove tracking parameters before a webpage is loaded.
  • storage – required to save your settings and allowlist.

Conclusion

By using this extension, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Since the extension does not collect or transmit any personal data, your privacy remains fully under your control.
